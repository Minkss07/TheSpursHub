Monte Carlo Simulation

Season Projection

The model, played forward 3,000 times

How This Works

The same prediction model used elsewhere on this site is applied to every remaining fixture in the season, giving a win/draw/loss probability for each one. The season is then simulated thousands of times — each time, every remaining match is randomly resolved according to those probabilities, and points are added up for all 20 teams.

Averaging across thousands of simulated seasons gives a projected final points total for every team, along with a realistic range of outcomes rather than one falsely precise number.

Simulates 3,000 full seasons — takes a few seconds.
