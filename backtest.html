Model Testing

Walk-forward backtest on a full season

What This Does

The prediction model is replayed against every match of the most recent completed Premier League season. For each match, the model is rebuilt from scratch using only matches that had already finished before that kickoff — it never sees the result it is being asked to predict, or anything that happened afterwards.

This is the difference between a model that looks accurate and one that is actually tested. Building ratings from a whole season and then "predicting" matches inside it would be grading its own homework.

Takes a few seconds — the model is rebuilt once per match.

Parameter Tuning

The model has three settings that could be tuned: how fast old matches lose influence, how strongly thin samples are pulled toward the league average, and the strength of the low-score correction. The sweep searches combinations of all three.

Crucially, it tunes on one season and reports results on a different season that played no part in the search. Choosing settings and reporting scores on the same data is the most common way model results get inflated — the gap between tuning and validation performance is the overfitting, made visible.

Runs ~30 full backtests. Takes longer than the single run above.
